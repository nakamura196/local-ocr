Available from version 0.1.11.
The Windows version is updated by the Microsoft Store. On a Mac, download the new version from [Releases](https://github.com/nakamura196/local-ocr/releases/latest).

### What it is

PP-OCRv6 is a text recognition model published by PaddlePaddle, the team behind PaddleOCR.
Local OCR includes its small version.

- **Languages**: Chinese, English, Japanese, and Latin-script languages such as French and German
- **Best for**: printed text
- **Size**: about 30 MB — much smaller than the other engines, so it downloads quickly
- **Speed**: an English page of 47 lines took about 1.2 seconds in our test
- **Vertical text**: lines are ordered right to left
- **Line positions**: boxes are drawn on the image

### How to use it

1. Open Settings with the gear icon at the top right
2. In the list of engines, press "Download" next to "PP-OCRv6 (print, multilingual)"
3. Choose it in the engine menu at the top, and read

### For pre-modern books, PaddleOCR-VL is better

We read 28 pages of woodblock-printed classical Chinese books with both PP-OCRv6 and PaddleOCR-VL.
PaddleOCR-VL read the main text more accurately. We found no character that only PP-OCRv6 got right.
PP-OCRv6 also picks up text outside the main text, such as the ruler and the library label in the photo.

For pre-modern books, keep using PaddleOCR-VL or NDL古典籍OCR Lite.
PP-OCRv6 suits printed and Western-language material when you want results quickly.

If unsure, use "Compare" to try both on your own material.
The video in the [other news item](2026-10-16-compare-diff-en.html) shows two engines reading the same page, with the differences highlighted.
